- SDL2 (SDL2.dll) - zlib License - https://www.libsdl.org/license.php

- Sun Valley ttk theme (sv-ttk) - MIT License - https://github.com/rdbende/Sun-Valley-ttk-theme

- python-qrcode - BSD License - https://github.com/lincolnloop/python-qrcode

The LGPL libraries are loaded as separate DLLs. To use your own builds of them,
put them in bin\ and rebuild with build.bat (source: mirror.pyw).
